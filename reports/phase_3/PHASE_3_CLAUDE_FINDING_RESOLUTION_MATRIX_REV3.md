## 3. Incorporation of Claude's Self-Corrections (§10)

Revision 3.0 formally adopts and preserves the three self-corrections made by Claude in Section 10 of the Re-Audit:

Revision 3.0 discloses this three-way discrepancy and places the resolution under DEC-019.
